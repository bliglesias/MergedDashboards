Loan document checklist

Client:
← Advisor Toolbox

Most lenders ask for the items below. Each lender's list is a little different, so bring what you have and we'll work through the rest. SBDC advising is free.

The request

How much you need: $
What the money will pay for (use of funds), with quotes
Your personal credit score (most banks and card issuers show it free)
How much of your own cash you'll put in: $

Forms

SBA Form 413, Personal Financial Statement, for each owner with 20% or more (sba.gov/document/sba-form-413-personal-financial-statement)
SBA Form 1919, Borrower Information Form, for SBA loans (sba.gov/document/sba-form-1919-borrower-information-form)

Financials

Business tax returns (often the last 3 years)
Personal tax returns for each owner (often the last 3 years)
Current profit and loss statement and balance sheet
Debt schedule: what the business owes now
Business plan with 12-month projections (startups: show how you got your numbers)

Business documents

LLC or entity documents, business licenses and registrations
Lease or purchase agreement for your location
List of assets you could offer as collateral (buildings, equipment, vehicles)
Resume for each owner
