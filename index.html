    Google Forms
      
          
    1. Responses tab, then Link to Sheets. Pick your master sheet.
      2. 
          
    2. Three-dot menu, then Get email notifications for new responses, so each submission is sent to you.
      3.

    Microsoft Forms
      
          
    1. Responses tab, then Open results in Excel. Keep that one workbook as the master sheet; new answers are added to it.
      2. 
          
    2. Three-dot menu, then Settings: turn on Get email notification of each response.
      3. 
          
    3. Need it in Google Sheets? Download the Excel file and import it, or use the Google Form route.
